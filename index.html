What Is Viral Dashboard?

Viral Dashboard is a web-based social media management platform that combines AI-assisted content creation, publishing, engagement, and performance tracking. Its purpose is to simplify repetitive tasks involved in maintaining multiple social media accounts.

Users begin by creating a brand workspace and connecting supported social accounts. They can enter business information, establish brand colors, fonts, logos, and voice preferences, then generate a 30-day content strategy. From there, they can edit captions, create graphics, schedule posts, manage comments, and review performance.

Although automation can reduce repetitive work, it does not eliminate the need for human judgment. Users should review captions, graphics, scheduled posts, customer replies, and analytics before relying on them.

How Does Viral Dashboard Work?

The platform organizes social media management into a connected workflow, moving from brand setup and content planning to creation, publishing, engagement, and performance analysis.

1

Step 1: Set Up Your Brand Workspace

Enter your business information and connect the social media accounts you want to manage. Add brand colors, fonts, logos, and voice preferences to maintain a consistent identity across your content.

2

Step 2: Generate a 30-Day Content Strategy

Use the AI-assisted planning feature to create a monthly content strategy. The resulting calendar contains planned post ideas that can be reviewed, edited, and organized before publication.

3

Step 3: Create Captions and Graphics

Open a planned post to generate caption variations using frameworks such as AIDA, HOOK, WIIFM, and AMP. The Creative Studio provides templates, stock photo access through Unsplash and Pexels, and saved brand kits.

4

Step 4: Schedule and Publish Content

Send completed posts to the composer and select connected social accounts. The platform advertises automatic formatting for different networks, including character limits, image dimensions, hashtags, and platform-specific previews.

5

Step 5: Manage Comments and Audience Engagement

The unified inbox brings supported comments and direct messages into a centralized interface. Saved replies, internal notes, tagging, assignments, and moderation rules help organize routine engagement activities.

6

Step 6: Review Analytics and Improve Your Strategy

Use cross-platform analytics to review audience, content, and engagement performance. Analytics Advanced advertises 90-day reporting history and CSV exports, while Intelligence Insight adds social listening, competitor tracking, and PDF reports.

Connect Accounts → Plan Content → Create Posts → Publish Everywhere → Analyze Results
